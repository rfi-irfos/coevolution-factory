## Index / parent line

CoEvolution AI runs 50 standing, interdisciplinary centers (not document
processors). Each center convenes a broad panel of experts — drawn from a
live 292-agent engine — spanning Legal, Risk & Compliance, AI Safety,
Finance, Operations, Security, and Executive leadership. A center is a
permanent body that convenes on a question, runs a what-if scenario
simulation, or produces a standing posture health-check.

## Operational framing (hedged per UIP gate)

- Day-to-day operation runs largely autonomously: signup, key issuance,
  panel sessions, billing and content run without a human in the loop.
- Human-owned: legal entity, banking, initial traffic, and the Laura gate
  on all public copy.
- The Observatory reports per-center session and euro totals only; we
  surface cashflow, we do not intermediate it.
- Centers re-optimize their own panels from real telemetry on a daily
  cadence, gated by Laura (no self-approval); the following cycle's data
  is what measures any improvement.

## Guardrails (no absolutes)

- We do not claim guaranteed outcomes, we do not publish fabricated case
  studies, and we do not use regulatory-certification language for the
  centers themselves.
- The engine is a decision-support tool; it surfaces expert perspectives,
  it does not replace qualified counsel.
