Why a team beats one smart model

A general model asked to find "anything wrong" has no incentive to stay in lane — it blends a security flaw into a legal nitpick into a wording complaint, all at the same confidence. We invert that.

01Lane-locked. Security only talks security. Legal only talks legal exposure. When two independently flag the same verbatim line, that's a real signal — not one model's mood.
02Verbatim or nothing. Every finding quotes the exact substring it reacted to. A finding whose evidence isn't literally in your text is dropped, not passed through on the model's word.
03Whitebox by design. After each pass every agent audits its own output and may rewrite its own prompt (versioned, append-only). Skill proposals wait for human review. EU AI Act transparency, operationalized.
04Entitlement-gated. A key is entitled to exactly the agents you licensed. A single-agent customer's key cannot reach the other 291. Licensing is enforced at the network boundary.
